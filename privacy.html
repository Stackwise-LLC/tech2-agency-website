Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026

2Tech Agency is operated by Stackwise LLC, a New Mexico, USA limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what personal information we collect and how we use it.

1. Information we collect

When you use the contact / quote form on our website, we collect the information you provide:

  • Your name
  • Your email address
  • Your WhatsApp number
  • The details of your project that you choose to include in your message

2. How we use it

This information is used strictly for project communication: replying to your request, preparing a quote, discussing requirements, and sending invoices or payment links. We never sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Sharing

We share data only with service providers needed to run our business (for example our email and messaging tools, and payment partners such as Stripe or PayPal when you pay an invoice), who may only use it to provide their service to us, or where required by law. Payment card details are handled solely by the payment partner and are never stored by us.

4. Retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the project and for legal, tax and accounting purposes, and apply reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect it.

5. Your rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information at any time. If you are in the European Economic Area, the UK or another jurisdiction with data protection laws, you may have additional rights, including to object to or restrict processing and to lodge a complaint with your local authority. Our legal basis for processing is the performance of a contract or steps taken at your request prior to a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

6. International transfers

We are based in the United States, so your information may be processed there.

2Tech Agency est exploitée par Stackwise LLC, société à responsabilité limitée du Nouveau-Mexique (États-Unis) (« nous »). La présente politique explique quelles données personnelles nous collectons et comment nous les utilisons.

1. Données collectées

Lorsque vous utilisez le formulaire de contact / de demande de devis de notre site, nous collectons les informations que vous fournissez :

  • Votre adresse e-mail
  • Votre numéro WhatsApp

2. Utilisation

Ces informations sont utilisées strictement pour la communication relative à votre projet : répondre à votre demande, établir un devis, échanger sur vos besoins et envoyer des factures ou liens de paiement. Nous ne vendons jamais vos données personnelles à des tiers.

Nous ne partageons vos données qu'avec les prestataires nécessaires à notre activité (par exemple nos outils de messagerie et d'e-mail, et les partenaires de paiement tels que Stripe ou PayPal lorsque vous réglez une facture), qui ne peuvent les utiliser que pour nous fournir leur service, ou lorsque la loi l'exige.
